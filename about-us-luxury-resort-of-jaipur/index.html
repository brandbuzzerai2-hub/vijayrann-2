Our Story

An escape from the busy world, in the lap of nature.

THE ROYAL SANCTUARY · KUKAS, JAIPUR

Have you ever wondered what an escape from your demanding life would feel like, in the lap of nature, with luxurious service and a peaceful environment? At Vijayran, the dream of a relaxed holiday where you are king comes true.

Set amidst emerald Aravalli peaks and quiet stone-paved courtyards at Kukas, our heritage estate brings together centuries-old Rajputana architecture, organic garden gastronomy and heartfelt, unhurried hospitality.

Kukas · Aravalli Foothills · 12 km North of Amber Fort

THE PALACE CHRONICLE · KUKAS, JAIPUR

CHAPTER I · A FORTRESS OF SOLITUDE

Old-world vibes meet the new age

Royal Quest Resorts developed The Vijayran Palace around a unique idea: an experience laden with luxury, world-class service and an atmosphere of solitude. Away from the worldly chaos, lose yourself in a fortress of solitude and magnificence. We await all our guests with open arms and excellent hospitality, because you deserve it.

Conceived as an intimate sanctuary far from urban clamour, every courtyard, archway, and colonnade invites guests to slow down and inhabit the poise of royal Rajasthan.

CHAPTER II · ARTISANAL HERITAGE

Hand-chiselled jharokhas & royal tapestries

Generations of Rajasthani stone-carvers, fresco painters, and textile masters shaped our cusped arches, pierced lattice screens, and mirror-inlaid chambers.

Sunlight filters through carved sandstone balconies onto hand-knotted carpets and period furnishings, turning every corridor into a living gallery of Rajputana craft.

CHAPTER III · PALATIAL COURTS & GARDENS

Illuminated amphitheatres & Mughal gardens

From the terraced stone stage of Rang Mahal to the geometric fountain courts of Char Bagh, seven distinct outdoor and indoor settings unfold across the estate.

Fragrant frangipani, bougainvillea cascades, and torchlit walkways frame intimate family gatherings and grand royal celebrations alike.
