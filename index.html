THE ROYAL SANCTUARY · KUKAS, JAIPUR

To stay at The Vijayran Palace is to live like royalty in the lap of the ancient Aravalli hills: soaring yellow-sandstone chhatris, hand-carved jharokha balconies and tranquil Mughal courtyards, infused with the warmth of true Rajasthani hospitality.

Cradled across lush, manicured lawns in Kukas—just twelve kilometres north of historic Amber Fort yet worlds removed from urban haste—our thirty-five private-pool villas and haveli suites pair regal Rajputana tapestries and period craftsmanship with effortless contemporary comfort.

Begin your mornings with guided yoga to birdsong, ride spirited Marwari horses along mountain trails, savour heirloom recipes harvested from our organic kitchen gardens, and gather beneath desert stars for folk music and candlelit feasts.

THE PALACE CHRONICLE · KUKAS, JAIPUR

CHAPTER I · IMPERIAL ARCHITECTURE

A heritage palace woven into the Aravalli hills

Royal Quest Resorts created The Vijayran Palace around a simple idea: luxury, world-class service and an atmosphere of solitude. It is where old-world vibes and a glorious past meet the new age, an amalgamation of culture, tradition and contemporary comfort.

Beyond the ceremonial yellow-sandstone Gajdwar portal, soaring chhatri cupolas, scalloped Mehrab colonnades, and hand-carved jharokhas rise in quiet majesty against the ancient Aravalli ridge.

CHAPTER III · SERENITY & REFLECTION

Turquoise stillness beneath palace chhatris

At the heart of the estate, the mirror-still waters of Ambar Taal reflect golden sandstone baradaris, white marble finials, and drifting mountain clouds from dawn until starlight.

Mornings unfold with birdsong yoga on dewy lawns and horseback rides through rural trails, giving way to lazy poolside afternoons and candlelit dining.
