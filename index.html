✦ Heritage Living · Rajputana Traditions ✦

Royal Pursuits of the Estate

Experience the timeless pastimes of Rajasthani royalty: morning rides into the Aravallis, majestic elephant interactions, vintage cycling through countryside trails, and solar yoga at dawn.

Equestrian Nobility

Marwari Stallion

Ride purebred Marwari steeds with distinctive lyre-shaped ears through scenic mountain trails and palace grounds.

Ceremonial Majesty

Gajraj in Motion

Gentle walks, feeding, and ceremonial evening processions with adorned palace elephants near ancient Amber.

Estate Exploration

Countryside Cycling

Crisp morning rides on vintage roadster bicycles along tranquil Kukas trails, organic farms, and bird reserves.

Celestial Wellness

Surya Vandana

Guided morning sun salutations and pranayama to birdsong on the dew-kissed lawns under the benevolent Rajasthan sun.

Activities & Experiences

Choose your pace: guided morning yoga, horseback rides into the hills, folk music under the stars, or peaceful moments in our gardens and courtyards.

Explore all activitiesExplore all activities

Morning Yoga

Guided morning yoga in our outdoor spaces, with birdsong and the pleasant Aravalli air waiting to embrace you.

Rajasthani Folk Evenings

An evening of music, dance and festivity: the Ravanahatha, Kalbeliya and Ghoomar, performed under the stars.

Ambar Taal Pool

An early-morning swim or a late evening by the pool. Water volleyball for the family and splashing fun for the children.

Elephant Ride & Lunch

A short drive into the scenic hills of Amer: feed the elephants, ride through the hills, spot birds and enjoy local delicacies.

Horse Riding

Well-trained, friendly horses for a safe and thrilling ride, from pony rides for children to carriage rides for couples.

Camel Ride

Experience the authenticity of Rajasthan on a camel ride through the Aravalli hills.

Open Jeep Safari

Off-road jeeps take you out to encounter the wild animals and birds of Rajasthan.

Heritage & Village Walks

Walk to nature and village life near Amber Fort and Nahargarh, and discover the traditions of rural Rajasthan.

Bird Watching

More than 150 varieties of birds in their natural habitat: a quiet joy for every nature lover.
