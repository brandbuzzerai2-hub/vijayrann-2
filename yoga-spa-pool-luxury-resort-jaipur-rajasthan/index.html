Activities

The “actual India”, at your doorstep.

A village safari, a majestic elephant ride, a ride on the finest horses, an open jeep safari, or simply gazing at the magnificent night sky. Set in a culturally vibrant region, the palace introduces its guests to an India rich in customs, traditions and beliefs.

Experiences

Choose your pace: active mornings, cultural evenings and quiet moments in between.

Morning Yoga

Guided morning yoga in our outdoor spaces, with birdsong and the pleasant Aravalli air waiting to embrace you.

Rajasthani Folk Evenings

An evening of music, dance and festivity: the Ravanahatha, Kalbeliya and Ghoomar, performed under the stars.

Ambar Taal Pool

An early-morning swim or a late evening by the pool. Water volleyball for the family and splashing fun for the children.

Elephant Ride & Lunch

A short drive into the scenic hills of Amer: feed the elephants, ride through the hills, spot birds and enjoy local delicacies.

Horse Riding

Well-trained, friendly horses for a safe and thrilling ride, from pony rides for children to carriage rides for couples.

Camel Ride

Experience the authenticity of Rajasthan on a camel ride through the Aravalli hills.

Open Jeep Safari

Off-road jeeps take you out to encounter the wild animals and birds of Rajasthan.

Heritage & Village Walks

Walk to nature and village life near Amber Fort and Nahargarh, and discover the traditions of rural Rajasthan.

Bird Watching

More than 150 varieties of birds in their natural habitat: a quiet joy for every nature lover.

Every evening

Rajasthani folk music under the stars

The land of the Ravanahatha, Kalbeliya and Ghoomar. The Vijayran Palace brings you an evening of dance, music and festivity filled with traditional Rajasthani folk songs and dances.

Slow mornings, wild afternoons

Begin with guided yoga to birdsong, spend the afternoon on horseback or on a jeep safari in the Aravallis, and end the day by the Ambar Taal pool as the sun sets over the hills.

Good to know

Plan your activities with our front office team on arrival.

Adventure activities carry inherent risk, and The Vijayran Palace accepts no liability arising from them. Please bring your own hat for jeep and camel safaris and keep children supervised.

Light cottons and linens for the day; a shawl or light jacket for winter evenings and early-morning safaris. Swimwear for the pool, comfortable walking shoes for heritage walks, a hat and sunscreen for jeep and camel safaris, and something festive for folk evenings.

Jaipur is at its best from October to March: warm, sunny days and cool evenings, with winter nights in December and January turning chilly. April to June is hot and dry, and the monsoon arrives from July to September, turning the Aravalli hills green.
