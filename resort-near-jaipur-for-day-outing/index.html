Good to know

Everything you need before you visit.

NH-8, Kukas, Village Kacherawala, Jaipur, Rajasthan 302028, India. The palace sits in the Aravalli hills at Kukas, off the Jaipur–Delhi highway (NH-48), about 12 km north of Amber Fort and a short drive from Jaipur city and Jaipur International Airport. Open in Google Maps.

Check-in is at 2:00 PM and check-out at 11:00 AM. 100% advance payment must be received 10 days before the check-in date. Cancellations made within 10 days of check-in are charged 100% and are non-refundable. Cash, credit card, debit card and bank transfer. Travellers' cheques and personal cheques are not accepted. Wi-Fi is available across the resort, and vegetarian and Jain meals are prepared on request.

Light cottons and linens for the day; a shawl or light jacket for winter evenings and early-morning safaris. Swimwear for the pool, comfortable walking shoes for heritage walks, a hat and sunscreen for jeep and camel safaris, and something festive for folk evenings.

Jaipur is at its best from October to March: warm, sunny days and cool evenings, with winter nights in December and January turning chilly. April to June is hot and dry, and the monsoon arrives from July to September, turning the Aravalli hills green.
